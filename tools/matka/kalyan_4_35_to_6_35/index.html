How Prediction Chance & Accuracy are Calculated:

This 30-day verification is executed through a strict Walk-Forward Validation protocol across 24 consecutive draws. For each day, the prediction engine was trained strictly on data prior to that day's draw (zero look-ahead bias), simulating real-time market prediction. Official declared results from DPBoss were then matched against the model's Top 3 Open digits, Top 3 Close digits, Top 5 Jodis, and 4-Ank OTC recommendations.

Mathematical Guardrail & Risk Management Notice

This automated system applies quantitative pattern recognition (Markov state transition matrices, recency exponential decay, and weekday seasonality priors).
